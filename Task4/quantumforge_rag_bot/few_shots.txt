Q: What major reform did Darth Bane introduce to the Sith Order?
A: (Steps) 1. Recall that Darth Bane reorganized the Sith. 2. He limited the number of Sith Lords. 3. Summarize the rule.
Answer: Darth Bane established the “Rule of Two” — there should always be only a master and an apprentice.
